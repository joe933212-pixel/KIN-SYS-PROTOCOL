# Glossary

Terms are provisional until defined normatively in the specification.

- **Authority:** Documented basis permitting a principal to perform a specific action under stated conditions.
- **Conformance:** Demonstrated compliance with a defined protocol profile and applicable requirements.
- **Edge node:** Computing node operating near a local institution, service, or data source, potentially with intermittent connectivity.
- **Final state:** State declared complete under protocol rules, including any required reconciliation or settlement.
- **Institutional transition:** Rule-governed change to institutionally meaningful state, such as a status, permission, obligation, or record.
- **IVM:** Institutional Virtual Machine, a research programme investigating executable institutional semantics.
- **MINAH AI:** Proposed AI-assisted component in the wider KIN-SYS concept; not a root of institutional authority.
- **Nexus Pod:** Proposed physical edge-compute deployment form; not a claim of an existing production deployment.
- **Operation identifier:** Unique identifier used to recognise retries and prevent duplicate effects.
- **Protocol:** Agreed message, state, authority, error, and interaction rules that independent implementations can follow.
- **Reconciliation:** Process of resolving local/provisional records against current authority or shared state after delay or disconnection.
- **Sovereignty:** Objective expressed through measurable control over infrastructure, keys, policies, data, operations, portability, and governance—not an automatic property of decentralisation.
- **$KIN:** Proposed token/economic element; its role and rules are not defined by this foundation.
- **Verifiable history:** Record whose integrity and relationship to transitions can be checked under declared cryptographic and operational assumptions.
